Details

Questions, answered.

Is it legal to scam my own staff?

It is when you authorise it in writing. The agreement defines which people, channels and dates are in scope, and we stay inside it. Check the terms with your own lawyer or insurer if you want extra comfort.

How is this different from phishing training?

Phishing tools send generic email templates and add an annual course. Mosey tests voice, video and multi-step social engineering personalised to your business, then feeds what worked into your defences.

Does any real money or data leave the business?

No. Simulated payment requests are stopped before funds move, and we do not collect real credentials or client records.

Will individual staff be named or blamed?

By default, findings are grouped by role and scam type. The aim is to fix approval steps and training, since a convincing cloned voice can fool anyone.

What happens after the first report?

We fix what broke: stronger verification workflows, defensive agents and targeted training for the scams that landed. Then we test again. Each round also improves how we attack the next business.

How is it priced?

You start with a fixed-price assessment that produces your report. Continuous testing and protection then moves to a subscription priced per employee or by tier. Exact prices are not set yet.

Is the product available now?

We are at an early stage and starting with a small group of pilot businesses, with a focus on Australia. Join the waitlist and we will contact you about a pilot.
